EMMIS ChefChats

Visual Plating Standards, Galley Oven Sequences, Allergen Matrices, and Caterer Sign-Off

Created by O'Sullivan Communications (OSC) as the operational execution module of the EMMIS ecosystem, EMMIS ChefChats bridges executive culinary design with onboard galley execution—delivering bilingual plating guides, heating curves, wine-pairing notes, and verified allergen data to flight kitchens and cabin crews worldwide.

MODULE PROFILE

Galley-to-Cabin Precision

  • Inputs: Approved dishes & wine notes from EMMIS CMS, chef plating photography
  • Core Outputs: Bilingual galley plating sheets, oven dwell guides, allergen matrices
  • Governance: Digital caterer sign-off workflows & station compliance logs
  • Related Solution: Inflight Menu Management & Galley Guides
CORE CAPABILITIES

Standardized Culinary Presentation & Crew Reference

Visual Plating Photography & Assembly Diagrams

Equips flight kitchens and cabin crews with high-resolution plating photography, garnish placement diagrams, and chinaware specifications so every course looks identical across international hubs.

Galley Oven Dwell Sequences & Heating Curves

Documents exact aircraft-specific convection and steam-oven temperatures, dwell times, and staging sequences—preventing overcooking at altitude and streamlining cabin service flow.

Verified Allergen & Dietary Matrices

Publishes synchronized ingredient and regulatory allergen disclosures alongside crew service notes—supporting passenger safety and international aviation compliance across every cabin tier.

Wine-Pairing Notes & Caterer Sign-Off Workflows

Pairs sommelier tasting guidance with digital caterer acknowledgment workflows—confirming that global flight kitchens have reviewed and signed off on seasonal specifications prior to launch.

ECOSYSTEM INTEGRATION

Closed-Loop Accountability From Schedule to Cabin

EMMIS ChefChats completes the end-to-end digital lifecycle—delivering flight-kitchen instructions derived from the exact schedule rules in EMMIS Order Maintenance, structured XML content in EMMIS CMS, and approved proofs in EMMIS MenuViewer.
