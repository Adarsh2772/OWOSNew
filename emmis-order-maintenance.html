EMMIS Order Maintenance

Automated Flight Schedule Ingestion, Print Allocation, and Global Station Logistics

Engineered by O'Sullivan Communications (OSC) as the operational entry point of the EMMIS ecosystem, EMMIS Order Maintenance transforms raw airline flight schedules into exact catering menu quantities, optimized multi-up press impositions, and automated worldwide shipping manifests.

MODULE PROFILE

Schedule-to-Press Control

  • Inputs: Airline flight schedules, aircraft cabin layouts, route language rules
  • Core Outputs: Exact station lot quantities, press imposition specs, shipping labels
  • Connected Modules: Feeds metadata directly into EMMIS CMS & EMMIS MenuViewer
  • Related Solution: Inflight Menu Management & Logistics
CORE CAPABILITIES

Precision Forecasting & Press-Room Optimization

Automated Flight Schedule Ingestion

Parses airline schedule feeds, departure frequencies, equipment types, and cabin load factors to eliminate manual spreadsheet calculations and seasonal volume guesswork.

Dynamic Station & Catering Lot Allocation

Calculates precise menu counts per departure station, outbound/inbound leg, and cabin tier—preventing costly station shortages while minimizing surplus print overruns.

Intelligent Multi-Up Press Imposition

Groups compatible route menus, trim sizes, and substrate stocks onto optimal press signatures—reducing plate changeovers, paper waste, and makeready lead times.

Automated Carton Labeling & Global Manifests

Generates barcoded carton labels, lot traceability records, and air/freight customs documentation for direct fulfillment from U.S. and U.K. hubs to global catering kitchens.

ECOSYSTEM INTEGRATION

Seamless Handoff Into Authoring & Proofing

Once EMMIS Order Maintenance locks route quantities, language targets, and template specs, those job parameters populate EMMIS CMS automatically—ensuring every menu variant is typeset, localized, and proofed in EMMIS MenuViewer against live departure schedules.
