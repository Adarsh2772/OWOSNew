THE EMMIS ECOSYSTEM · PROPRIETARY PUBLISHING & LOGISTICS INTELLIGENCE

Our Platform: The Proprietary EMMIS Ecosystem for Inflight Menu Management and Publishing

Purpose-built by O'Sullivan Communications (OSC), EMMIS unifies flight schedule analytics, structured content authoring, digital menu inspection, and galley execution into one high-reliability enterprise platform.

Where traditional operations rely on disconnected spreadsheets and manual vendor handoffs, EMMIS integrates real-time schedule ingestion, single-source content authoring, secure digital inspection, and galley crew coordination into a unified, zero-defect workflow.

CONNECTED DIGITAL WORKFLOW

From Schedule to Service

Every seasonal menu cycle involves dozens of handoffs across airlines, catering kitchens, linguists, and print facilities. EMMIS keeps that entire workflow connected on a single enterprise platform.

Raw flight schedules enter EMMIS Order Maintenance to calculate exact print volumes and press imposition rules. Reusable modular content flows through EMMIS CMS for collaborative review, automated typesetting, and translation memory synchronization—rendering simultaneously to press-ready print files, EMMIS MenuViewer digital portals, and EMMIS ChefChats galley guides.

OPERATIONAL IMPACT

Enterprise Outcomes and Proven Efficiencies

70% Faster Cycle Rotations

Reusable modular content architecture allows culinary teams to rotate seasonal cycles in hours rather than weeks, dramatically reducing production lead time.

Zero Under-Catering

Dynamic forecasting algorithms match menu production to aircraft configurations and catering load factors, preventing station shortages and costly reprints.

Reduced Press Waste

Automated prepress imposition combines shared food, language, and layout components across routes, maximizing press-sheet usage and reducing plate setups.

Audit-Proof Consistency

Direct translation memory integration ensures culinary, allergen, and brand terminology remain consistent across all routes and languages.

PLATFORM ARCHITECTURE

Core Applications Within the EMMIS Ecosystem

01 · Schedule & Logistics

EMMIS Order Maintenance

Transform raw flight schedules into exact catering menu allocations, optimized multi-up press impositions, and automated global station shipping manifests.

02 · Content & Localization

EMMIS CMS

Decouple culinary and technical authoring from layout geometry—manage modular XML components, synchronize Translation Memory, and automate print and seatback IFE outputs.

03 · Digital Proofing & Archive

EMMIS MenuViewer

Provide airline brand managers and catering leads with instant, SSO-protected cloud access to active and historical flying menus filtered by flight, route, or cabin class.

04 · Galley & Cabin Execution

EMMIS ChefChats

Equip flight kitchens and cabin crews with visual plating photography, galley oven dwell sequences, wine-pairing guidance, allergen matrices, and caterer sign-off workflows.
