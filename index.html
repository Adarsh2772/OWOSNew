INSTITUTIONAL PEDIGREE

Award-Winning Heritage & Dual-Continent Scale

Founded in 1973, O'Sullivan Communications (OSC) designs, manages, translates, prints, and distributes over 65 million inflight menus annually from coordinated U.S. and U.K. facilities—serving premier international airlines alongside Fortune 500 leaders across consumer products, electronics, and pharmaceuticals.

SINGLE-SOURCE ACCOUNTABILITY

Translation and Print Production Under One Roof

Dividing editorial localization, desktop typesetting, and commercial printing across disconnected vendors introduces version drift and costly delays. OSC integrates native linguists, creative designers, proprietary EMMIS automation, prepress engineering, and global fulfillment under single-point accountability.

ENTERPRISE SOLUTIONS

Built for High-Stakes Publishing Across Global Industries

Inflight Menu Management & Crew Reference Documentation

Turnkey lifecycle management—or flexible à la carte logistics and production—for printed cabin menus, airline wine guides, Flight Attendant Reference Sheets, EMMIS ChefChats galley guides, and synchronized seatback IFE digital feeds.

Enterprise Translation, Technical Manuals & Commercial Publishing

Multilingual localization, centralized translation memory, native editorial proofing, and desktop publishing across 75+ language variants for complex technical manuals, regulatory filings, and dual-continent commercial print production.

PROPRIETARY TECHNOLOGY

Uniting EMMIS Order Maintenance, EMMIS CMS, EMMIS MenuViewer, and EMMIS ChefChats in one digital ecosystem to ingest flight schedules, automate modular composition, enable cloud menu inspection, and deliver verified plating guides to flight kitchens and cabin crews.
