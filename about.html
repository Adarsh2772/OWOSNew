CORPORATE OVERVIEW & HERITAGE

O'Sullivan Communications (OSC)

Award-Winning Operational Excellence in Multilingual Publishing and Inflight Programs

Founded in 1973 as a specialized design firm delivering editorial services, translation, and typography for the hospitality and travel industries, O'Sullivan Communications (OSC) pioneered a true One World, One Stop production model built on departure-critical precision and dedicated client partnership. Today, OSC designs, manages, translates, prints, and ships more than 65 million inflight menus annually for premier international airlines, while supporting Fortune 500 leaders across consumer products, electronics, pharmaceuticals, and health and beauty.

SINGLE-SOURCE ACCOUNTABILITY

An Award-Winning Legacy of Single-Source Accountability

Rather than outsourcing production stages across fragmented vendors, O'Sullivan Communications (OSC) unites every step of complex publishing and print logistics under one roof—eliminating version drift, protecting brand consistency, and securing dependable on-time delivery.

Creative & Linguistic Precision

Dedicated account management, native culinary and technical linguists, editorial proofreaders, and in-house desktop publishing specialists across 75+ language variants.

Proprietary EMMIS Automation

Global Print & Fulfillment

High-resolution prepress engineering, commercial print manufacturing, and direct global distribution from coordinated U.S. and U.K. operations.
